# Ticket Status and Resolution

Authorized employees can use **Change status** on the ticket detail screen.
The available choices are restricted to valid next states and the user's
permissions.

Pending states include Pending Client, Pending Internal, and Pending Vendor.
A ticket entering Resolved requires both a resolution summary and a root-cause
summary. Those values remain visible in the ticket context and are retained in
status/audit history. Closed is available only after Resolved or Client
Confirmation; reopening also follows the controlled workflow.

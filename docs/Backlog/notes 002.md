9. DN | HR Page and KPI Submission Workflow | The KPI workspace has Team Workflow, HR Finalized KPIs, and History & Comparison tabs. Team Leaders can generate evidence, repeatedly save editable drafts, complete ratings, and submit individually or select all eligible quarter drafts for atomic bulk submission. Each bulk handoff creates a versioned container named `Support KPIs Quarter {quarter}_{year}`; every selected KPI must be complete, in scope, Draft, and belong to the selected quarter, so partial submission cannot occur. The container and its employee count/history are visible to HR. Submission automatically queues one deduplicated email notification to active HR-profile recipients and the applicable employees’ reporting/department managers. Managers can submit containers only with `kpi.batch.submit`; container viewing, cutoff administration, late override, Team Leader submission/editing, HR queue viewing, and HR acknowledgement use separate permissions. KPI generation and container submission are governed in `Africa/Cairo` by a configurable policy: the final seven calendar days of each quarter by default, or the final seven days of every month. Outside the window, only an authorized override with a documented reason of at least 10 characters succeeds and is audited. HR sees submitted KPIs only and records an idempotent “Viewed by HR” acknowledgement. Reopening preserves an immutable revision, returns the KPI to Draft, and resubmission increments its version and resets HR acknowledgement. Historical review, engineer comparison summaries/trend curves, per-submission Excel, and comparison Excel are included in English and Arabic. Migrations `0163_kpi_submission_workflow`, `0164_kpi_cutoff_policy`, and `0165_kpi_submission_batches` implement the lifecycle and controls. Verified by clean migration through 0165, Ruff, strict MyPy, 335 backend tests, ESLint, TypeScript, 47 frontend tests, and production build.
